### Jazz & Kare

Inside Sunny Coral time moved differently Rai-san is steering his Japanese curry in the kitchen behind,  his jazz playlist drifting to the café, warm and grainy the kind of song that wraps around you before you even notice that. I sat there scratching his cat's neck (Kotora 子虎 - Small tiger) . Eating this kare, felt like what jazz must tastes like. 

Deep notes, bright notes, little surprises tucked between bytes. Outside, the island's wind shimmered, but inside this small hut, it was all mellow rhythm and gentle conversations. For a moment, I wasn't just traveling, I was drifting, letting a bowl of curry and a few jazz chords tell me everything I needed to know about being alive and far from home in the best possible way.
